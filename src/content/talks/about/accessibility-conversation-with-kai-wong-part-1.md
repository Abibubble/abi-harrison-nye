[Kai Wong](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kwong4a11y/) is an accessibility advocate, on a mission to make the digital world more accessible for disabled people.

I first came across Kai at axe-con 2025, where they gave a talk called [“Influencing Without Authority: A Pep Talk For Accessibility Advocates”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N7-aXoqt0kg). When I got the chance to talk with Kai, I jumped at it. This is part 1 of our conversation, for Disability Pride Month.
